Quick Rules

Single piece: 1 square any direction, empty only, cannot capture.

Phalanx (2+ pieces in a straight line, no gaps): moves along its axis up to N squares (N = phalanx length).

Capture: Phalanx of N captures an enemy phalanx of <N pieces directly ahead.

Win: At the start of your turn, more pieces on opponent's back row than they have on yours.

    Rules Cheat Sheet

    Board: 14 × 12. White: rows 11–12 (bottom). Black: rows 1–2 (top). White moves first.

    Single piece: 1 square any direction, empty only. Cannot capture.

    Phalanx (2+ pieces in a straight line, no gaps): moves along its own axis. Max distance = phalanx length. Any sub-line can be used as a phalanx.

    Capture: Phalanx of N hits a smaller enemy phalanx (<N) head-on → all removed.

    Win: At the START of your turn, more pieces on opponent's back row than they have on yours.

    Tip: Diagonal phalanxes are hard to block. Length = power.
